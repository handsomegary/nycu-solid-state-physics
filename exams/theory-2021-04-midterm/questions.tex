\section*{1. (45\%) True or false. Explain for your answer.}
Each item is worth 3\%.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item The sites of carbon atoms in a diamond crystal form a Bravais lattice.
\item Consider two sets of vectors in two-dimensional space,
\[
\{\mathbf t_1=(2a,-b),\ \mathbf t_2=(-a,b)\}\quad\text{and}\quad
\{\mathbf s_1=(a,3b),\ \mathbf s_2=(-2a,-b)\}.
\]
The two sets of vectors are the primitive vectors spanning the same Bravais lattice.
\item Two different Bravais lattices having the same point group belong to the same crystal system.
\item Two atoms of the same type in a simple crystal must be connected by a translation vector of the underlying Bravais lattice.
\item For all three-dimensional Bravais lattices, the product of the Wigner-Seitz cell and the Brillouin zone is constant.
\item According to the Born-von Karman boundary condition, the number of allowed $\mathbf k$ points in the Brillouin zone is equal to the number of the primitive cells in the crystal. Therefore, the density of $\mathbf k$ points is proportional to the crystal volume.
\item According to the Bloch theorem, an eigenfunction of the crystal Hamiltonian in a unit cell can be determined from that in one of other unit cells.
\item For a many-electron system, the ground-state energy in the Hartree-Fock theory must be lower than that in the Hartree theory.
\item The determinantal wavefunction formed with the spin-orbitals that satisfy the Hartree-Fock equations is the eigenfunction of the interacting many-electron Hamiltonian.
\item In the Hartree-Fock theory, the determinantal wavefunction formed with the spin-orbitals that satisfy the Hartree-Fock equations is the best wavefunction for the ground state of the interacting many-electron Hamiltonian.
\item The energy of the normal ground state of a homogeneous electron gas is always lower than that of the fully spin-polarized ground state.
\item In the Hohenberg-Kohn functional, the electron density depends on the external potential.
\item The Kohn-Sham orbitals form the determinantal wavefunction of the ground state of an interacting many-electron system.
\item For an interacting many-electron ground state, the electron density can be expressed by $\sum_{i=1}^N|\phi_i(\mathbf r)|^2$ and the kinetic energy can be expressed by $\sum_{i=1}^N\langle\phi_i|\mathbf p^2/(2m)|\phi_i\rangle$, where $\phi_i$ are the Kohn-Sham orbitals.
\item The Kohn-Sham equations determine exactly the ground-state electron density and the electronic ground-state energy.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{4. (20\%)}
Consider the two-dimensional crystal of graphite, which has two carbon atoms per unit cell at positions
\[
\mathbf d_1=0,\qquad \mathbf d_2=a(0,1/\sqrt3).
\]
The primitive translation vectors for the underlying two-dimensional hexagonal lattice are
\[
\mathbf t_1=a(1/2,\sqrt3/2),\qquad
\mathbf t_2=a(-1/2,\sqrt3/2).
\]
Graphite has two $\pi$ bands with dispersion
\[
\epsilon_{\mathbf k}=\epsilon_p\pm\gamma|F_{\mathbf k}|,
\]
where $\epsilon_p$ is a constant, $\gamma>0$, and
\[
|F_{\mathbf k}|^2=1+4\cos(k_xa/2)\cos(\sqrt3k_ya/2)
                   +4\cos^2(k_xa/2).
\]
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item (5\%) Find a set of primitive vectors of the reciprocal lattice.
\item (10\%) Identify if or not symmetry points $\Gamma$ ($\mathbf k=0$), $Q$ [$\mathbf k=(2\pi/a)(1/2,1/(2\sqrt3))$], and $P$ [$\mathbf k=(2\pi/a)(2/3,0)$] of the first Brillouin zone are critical points. What type of critical point are they?
\item (5\%) Express the density of states contributed from the states in a small region containing a critical point you choose as you like from those in (b).
\end{enumerate}
