\chapter*{Editorial note}
This collection reconstructs supplied Solid State Theory and Solid State Physics examination pages. Each chapter preserves the printed course title, date, question numbers, and weights. These papers come from different courses and years; this is an independent study archive rather than an official course publication.

Legible printed questions are transcribed in English. Supplied answers, handwritten annotations, and grading marks are treated as reference material, not as part of the questions. Two source groups have missing or cropped material: only the first printed page of the May 2014 examination is available, and another fragment has no visible date or initial problem setup. Their coverage is explicitly identified. Missing question text is not invented. Printed weights are retained even when they do not sum to 100\%.

Read this volume alongside the separate \textit{Detailed Solutions}. All equations and reconstructed figures have editable \LaTeX{} sources.
